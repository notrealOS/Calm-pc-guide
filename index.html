CALM LAUNCHER

YOUR PC AS THE AI

Optional. Calm works on its own. A computer you own gives you a bigger model for the held digest, ASK, circle-to-search answers and reading web links. About fifteen minutes, once. Nothing goes to us or to any cloud: your phone talks straight to your PC, and if the PC is off the phone quietly does it by itself.

1

TAILSCALE ON BOTH

Install Tailscale (free) on the PC and on the phone from tailscale.com/download and sign in to the same account on both. It gives each device a private address that starts with 100. and works from anywhere. Nothing needs opening on your router.

Check: the Tailscale app on the phone shows Connected.

2

OLLAMA ON THE PC

Install Ollama from ollama.com (Windows, Mac or Linux). It runs the language model. You do not need to download a model by hand: the next step picks one.

3

THE NOTREAL SERVER

Download the file (Python 3.8 or newer is the only requirement, no packages):

DOWNLOAD notreal-server.py

Open a terminal in the folder where it landed and run it once:

python3 notreal-server.py --install

It picks a model (a small one of about 3 GB if you have none), starts itself every time you log in, and listens on your Tailscale address, port 8097. On Windows use python instead of python3.

To run it in the window instead, without installing: python3 notreal-server.py. To remove it later:

python3 notreal-server.py --uninstall
4

TELL THE PHONE

Find the PC's Tailscale address: the 100.x.x.x number in the Tailscale app on the PC, or in a terminal there:

tailscale ip -4

In Calm: Settings, SERVERS AND ACCOUNTS. Type it in YOUR PC / AI SERVER, no port, and tap TEST PC.

THE PHONE SHOULD SAYPC ANSWERS.

The setup plan in the launcher ticks CONNECT YOUR OWN PC AS THE AI by itself.

WHAT YOU GET

THIS SERVER DOES

  • Summaries and the held digest
  • ASK and search-bar answers
  • The circle assistant
  • Reading a pasted web link

THE PHONE DOES ITSELF

  • Voice typing
  • Photo enhance and pictures
  • Web search
  • Hub checks

These answer "not enabled" on this simple server, and the phone either does them on its own or leaves them off.

ALREADY RUN YOUR OWN AI?

Use any OpenAI-compatible server (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, vLLM) instead. In SERVERS AND ACCOUNTS tap ADVANCED, type its full address with the port in AI SERVER ADDRESS (for example http://192.168.1.20:11434) and the model name in AI MODEL NAME, then tap TEST AI.

IF IT SAYS "NO ANSWER FROM THE PC"

  • The PC is on and awake. A sleeping PC does not answer.
  • Tailscale shows connected on both the phone and the PC.
  • The address is the PC's 100.x.x.x number, with no port.
  • The server is running. On the PC open http://127.0.0.1:8097/health in a browser: it should answer. If not, run python3 notreal-server.py in a terminal and read what it prints.
  • The firewall on the PC allows port 8097 from Tailscale.

Still stuck? In Calm's setup plan, tap COPY SETUP BRIEF and paste it into any AI assistant for help. The brief holds your answers, the plan and your phone model. No passwords, keys or messages.
